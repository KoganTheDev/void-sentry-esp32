uint32_t aiming::AimPolicy::home_remaining_ms ( uint32_t now_ms) const

Milliseconds until the pending return Home fires: home_delay_ms minus the time since the target was last seen, minimum 0 and wraparound-safe. 0 unless the last update() saw Idle or Tentative, a target was seen and Home has not fired yet.

The timer runs through Tentative (update() leaves the last-seen time alone then), although Home itself can only fire on Idle.
