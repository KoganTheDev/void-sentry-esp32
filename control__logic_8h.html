Detailed Description

The controller's decisions: button gestures, HUD requests, modes, calibration and jog arbitration (pure C++20, natively tested).

Controller (lib/controller) is the device glue around this class: it owns the clock, the HUD inbox and its spinlock, NVS, logging and the published status, and implements control::ControlPorts over the turret, the detection module and the joystick. ControlLogic makes every read and command through the ports at the point where the decision needs it, so the joystick ADC is read only on a jog pass, the HUD jog's clock is read inside the inbox lock, and the turret's latest-wins mailbox receives its writes in decision order. Single-threaded: run() belongs to the loop task.
