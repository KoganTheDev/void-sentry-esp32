Detailed Description

Maps a target's image position to turret angles (pure C++20, natively tested).

The camera is fixed (not on the turret), so aiming is open-loop: pan depends linearly on image x and tilt on image y. The map comes from a 2-point joystick calibration, or from default_map() (camera next to the turret, OV3660 field of view) until one exists. Angles: pan degrees from home (positive = right), tilt servo degrees (larger = up).
