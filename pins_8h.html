Detailed Description

Single source of truth for every GPIO the turret uses.

Board: Freenove ESP32-WROVER CAM. With the camera attached only GPIO 0, 2, 12, 13, 14, 15, 32 and 33 are free, and the turret needs all eight. The static_asserts below turn a bad assignment into a build error instead of a board that corrupts PSRAM or refuses to boot.
