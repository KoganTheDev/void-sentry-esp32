Detailed Description

Slot bookkeeping for camera frames: who may write which slot, and RAII leases for readers.

Pure C++17, no platform headers, so it is unit tested natively (with ASan/UBSan in CI). NOT thread-safe on its own: on the device, FrameStore serializes every call with a mutex.

A slot is Free, Writing or Published. One Published slot is the latest. A Published slot that is no longer the latest becomes Free when its last reader releases it, so a slot with readers is never rewritten.
