    The joystick on the turret always wins over the HUD. Short press: AI / Manual. Hold 1.5 s: start or cancel calibration. Short press while calibrating: record a point.

    The red reticle shows where the turret points; the corner brackets mark the tracked target. In Manual, tap or drag on the video to aim. Fire is a HUD effect only: it hits when the reticle is on the target.

    Allow automatic firing?

    In AI mode the turret will fire by itself whenever it holds a target for 0.8 s, without you pressing FIRE.

    Arm it only when no people or pets are in front of the turret. It turns off again if the link drops or the page reloads.
