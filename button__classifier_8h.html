Detailed Description

Turns a raw push-button level into Short / Long press events (pure C++17, natively tested).

Debounces the raw level (it must be stable for debounce_ms). A press released before long_ms after it was confirmed is Short (reported on release). A press still held long_ms after confirmation is Long (reported once, at that moment; the release then reports nothing). Call update() every loop pass with millis().
